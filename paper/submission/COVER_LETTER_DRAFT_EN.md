Dear Editor,

Please consider “What Does Timing Add to Size? Probabilistic Forecasts of Recorded Cascade Growth on Two Archives” for consideration as a Research Article in IEEE Access.

Early cascade size is a strong forecasting baseline. This study quantifies the additional value of seven compact timing summaries within three established distributional forecasting procedures on the Weibo and SEISMIC archives. Source-specific, validation-selected models are evaluated on 10,000 held-out roots at three prefix durations. All six primary within-family information contrasts favor timing under the stated conditional, multiplicity-adjusted inference. Strong conditional empirical references place the magnitudes in context.

The manuscript also reports count-calibration limitations, duplicate sensitivity, all exploratory prefix-size strata, and a separately specified post-test independent-noise control. It distinguishes these supplementary results from the primary comparisons. It does not claim a new forecasting algorithm, superiority over unimplemented specialized cascade models, or cross-platform transfer. The technical supplement preserves numerical eligibility failures, protocol history and complete supplementary tables.

This research received no funding. The author declares no conflicts of interest.
